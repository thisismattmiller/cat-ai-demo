## Applies when

- The class number is in K (law), J1-J75 (official gazettes and certain congressional documents), JX1-JX1304 (obsolete subclass, international law), JX2000-end (obsolete subclass, international relations), or the item carries the call number LAW.
- The resource is a legal material entered under a corporate body or conference heading and several publications share the same Cutter and date (work letters needed) [sec. 4].

## Rules

1. [sec. 1.a] **Materials classed in J1-J75.** Shelflist according to regular shelflisting procedures.
2. [sec. 1.a] **JX1-JX1304, JX2000-end.** No new titles are classed here. Copies and added volumes are still added to already-established bibliographic records.
3. [sec. 1.a] **K.** Shelflist according to regular shelflisting procedures for law and legal materials.
4. [sec. 1.e.k] Congressional reports, when cataloged, class in KF31+.
5. [sec. 3] **Materials classed in LAW.** New materials are not classed in LAW. Materials classed in LAW are reclassed as time and staffing permit; almost all of LAW has been reclassed to J1-J9, JZ, and K.
6. [sec. 4] **Publications entered under corporate body, including conference publications.** If all single work letters have been used, the general procedure is to add double letters to the date in the call number (cf. G 220, G 230). For legal materials, if all single work letters have been used, add double letters to the date in the call number starting with `za`. If all double work letters have been used, add triple letters to the date in the call number starting with `zza`.

## Examples

Legal material under a corporate body; single work letters exhausted at `y`, `z`, then double letters starting at `za` [sec. 4]:

```
KF27 .I549 1996y
KF27 .I549 1996z
KF27 .I549 1996za
KF27 .I549 1996zb
```

Double work letters exhausted at `zz`, then triple letters starting at `zza` [sec. 4]:

```
KF27 .I549 1996zy
KF27 .I549 1996zz
KF27 .I549 1996zza
KF27 .I549 1996zzb
```

## Pitfalls

- The work-letter sequence for legal materials differs from G 220/G 230: in K the single letters run through `z` and the double letters start at `za` (not `ba` as in G 230 sec. 2.c), and triple letters start at `zza`.
- G 220's corporate-body Cuttering conventions are not applied in Class K; there each corporate name is given a separate Cutter number (G 220 Background).
- Do not class new titles in JX1-JX1304, JX2000-end, or LAW.
