## Applies when

- The topic is represented by a single class number whose schedule entry (or the shelflist)
  reserves an ".A" Cutter for periodicals, e.g. `.A1 Periodicals. Societies. Serials` /
  `.A3-Z General works`.
- The work is a periodical/serial on such a topic.

## Rules

1. Before 1971 ".A" Cutters were assigned to periodicals under a particular topic if the
   schedule for the topic did not explicitly provide for periodicals. That arrangement was
   controlled only by shelflist guidecards and was not published in the schedules. After 1971
   the practice was discontinued. [Background]
2. Establish an ".A" Cutter for periodicals under a topic only if that arrangement already
   exists in the shelflist or if the topic is represented by a single number and other forms
   have already been provided with ".A" Cutters.
3. When this arrangement exists in the shelflist, continue to use it and establish it in the
   schedule.

## Examples

A single class number with the periodicals .A Cutter established in the schedule:
```
462.A1   Periodicals. Societies. Serials
   .A3-Z  General works
```
(A periodical on the topic is Cuttered `.A1` plus its work mark; general works are Cuttered
in the range `.A3-Z`.)

## Pitfalls

- Do not create a new ".A" periodicals Cutter under a topic that has never had one; new
  periodicals numbers are whole or decimal numbers (F 210 sec. 3).
- The earlier requirement to cancel ".A" Cutters existing only in the shelflist when fewer
  than "several serials" were involved has been superseded [Background].
