## Applies when
- The resource is a bibliography or catalog of dissertations or theses (not an individual dissertation).
- Decide whether it is limited to a topic, to an individual university, or neither.

## Rules
1. General bibliographies and catalogs. Class general bibliographies and catalogs of dissertations or theses limited neither to a topic nor to an individual university in Z5053+. Do not assign an alternative number. [sec. 1]
2. Dissertations from an individual university. Class general bibliographies and catalogs of dissertations or theses from an individual university not limited to a topic in Z5055+ and assign an alternative number in AS11+. [sec. 2]
3. Dissertations on a specific topic. Class bibliographies and catalogs of dissertations or theses on a topic with the topic in class Z and assign an alternative number from the regular classes. [sec. 3]

## Pitfalls
- Only the general, unlimited bibliographies (Z5053+) get no alternative number; the university-limited and topic-limited ones each get one (AS11+ or the regular class). [secs. 1-3]
