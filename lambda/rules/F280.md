summary: >-
  A "General works" caption is provided whenever a topic has subtopics or form numbers
  indented under it; the General works number carries the topic as a whole, and any
  "Including" note belongs to the topical caption above it, not to the General works line.
---

## Applies when

- The schedule shows an unnumbered topical caption followed by a numbered "General works"
  caption and numbered subtopics or form numbers, and the work is on the topic as a whole.
- An "Including" note appears under the topical caption above a General works number and the
  work is on the included subtopic.

## Rules

1. General rule. Provide General works captions for a topic whenever there are any subtopics
   or form numbers indented hierarchically under the topic. The layout is: an unnumbered
   topical caption, followed by a numbered General works caption and numbered subtopics.
   [Background; sec. 1]
2. "Including" notes. Do not add any other topic to a General works caption line. Do not
   provide an "including" note or other notes describing usage under a General works caption.
   If such notes are needed, locate them under the topical caption under which the General
   works number is indented. [sec. 2]

## Examples

Topic with a form number and a General works number [sec. 1]:
```
          Animal products
1950        Periodicals, societies, etc.
1955        General works
```

Topic with a subtopic [sec. 1]:
```
          Casting
454         General works
454.2       Fly casting
```

Topic with a see reference and a subtopic carrying its own Including note [sec. 1]:
```
          Building and construction industry
              For building laws, see KJC6155
6786        General works
6788        Contracts and specifications (IV)
              Including liability
```

Topic with a geographic subarrangement [sec. 1]:
```
          Research. Experimentation
1027.8      General works
1027.9.A-Z  By region or country, A-Z
```

Topic with nested geographic hierarchy, each level with its own General works [sec. 1]:
```
          Rural renewal
435         General works
            By region or country
              United States
440             General works
441.A-Z         By region or state, A-Z
442.A-Z         Local, A-Z
443.A-Z       Other regions or countries, A-Z
```

Including note placed under the topical caption, not on the General works line [sec. 2]:
```
          Exceptional children
            Including subnormal and supernormal children
3950        Periodicals. Societies
3955        Congresses. Conferences, etc.
3965        General works
```
not:
```
          Exceptional children
3950        Periodicals. Societies
3955        Congresses. Conferences, etc.
3965        General works. Subnormal and supernormal children
```
and not:
```
          Exceptional children
3950        Periodicals. Societies
3955        Congresses. Conferences, etc.
3965        General works
              Including subnormal and supernormal children
```

## Pitfalls

- An Including note under the topical caption governs the whole topic (all its form numbers
  and General works), not just one line [sec. 2].
- In nested geographic developments, each level (e.g. United States) has its own General works
  number distinct from the topic's General works [sec. 1 example].
