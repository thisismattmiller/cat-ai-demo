## Applies when
- The class number (or a table applied to it) has the caption "By region or country, A-Z", "By country, A-Z", "By region or state, A-Z" or an equivalent, and a geographic Cutter must be supplied.
- The place to be Cuttered is a region larger than a country, a region crossing national boundaries, a country, a region or political division inside a country, a constituent country of Great Britain, an island, or a historical jurisdiction.
- The place has changed its name or its national affiliation since the period the work covers.
- The caption carries an "Under each" subarrangement (.x General works, .x2 Local, A-Z, etc.).

## Rules
1. Caption. "By region or country, A-Z" is the caption that provides for a Cutter arrangement of individual regions or countries. [sec. 1]
2. Older captions. "By country, A-Z" still appears in many places in the schedules; interpret it to mean "By region or country, A-Z" unless the schedule has specific instructions to the contrary or specific provisions for regions. [sec. 2]
3. Assign a Cutter number only for a country, a region larger than a country, or a region that crosses national boundaries. [sec. 3]
   - a. Regions: assign a Cutter number for a region that is larger than a single country or that crosses national boundaries. [sec. 3.a]
   - b. Countries and regions within a country: under a caption "By region or country, A-Z" (or its equivalent) that includes no subarrangement, use the country Cutter number both for the country as a whole and for any region or political division located entirely within the country. [sec. 3.b]
   - c. Country names modified by an adjective (e.g., "Brazil, North"): if the class number represents only the one country, assign a Cutter corresponding to the adjective. If the class number represents more than one country, assign a Cutter for the country only; do not bring out the region. [sec. 3.c]
4. Use current place names. Base the Cutter number on the latest form of a place name unless different provisions are printed in the schedule. [sec. 4]
5. Use current political status and name. If a place was formerly located within the boundaries of another country, assign a Cutter for the country in which it is currently situated and use its present name, even if the work describes its former status and refers to the place by an earlier name. [sec. 5]
6. Historical jurisdictions/entities. Assign a Cutter using the established form of any historical jurisdiction or entity that existed in the past and whose former territories are not encompassed by a present-day country. [sec. 6]
7. Great Britain.
   - a. Treat the constituent countries of Great Britain (England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland) as localities of Great Britain unless the schedule has special provisions or instructions to the contrary. That is, assign a Cutter for Great Britain, not for England, Wales, Scotland or Northern Ireland. [sec. 7.a]
   - b. Unless the schedule says otherwise, if there is provision for local subarrangement, treat any level below Great Britain as a local. [sec. 7.b]
8. Islands.
   - a. An island located near a country and treated as part of it: assign the Cutter for the country. [sec. 8.a]
   - b. An island or island group that is isolated from its parent country, or is itself a country: assign a Cutter under its own name. [sec. 8.b]
9. Subarrangement. When an "Under each" subarrangement provides for an alphabetical listing of localities, use localities only under individual countries. Do not use the provision for localities under regions. Other parts of the subarrangement may be used under regions. [sec. 9]
10. Shelflisting. For shelflisting information and the Regions and Countries Table, see G 300. [sec. 10]

## Tables
Types of "Under each" subarrangement seen in the schedules [sec. 9]:

| Caption | Subarrangement |
|---|---|
| By region or country, A-Z — Under each: | .x General works; .x2 Local, A-Z |
| By region or country, A-Z — Under each (local numbers used under country only): | .x General works; .x2 Local, A-Z |
| By region or country, A-Z — Under each (local numbers used under country only): | .x Periodicals. Societies. Serials; .x2 General works; .x25 Local, A-Z; .x3 By company, A-Z |

## Examples
Regions larger than a country or crossing national boundaries [sec. 3.a]:
```
Africa, North        .A355  [encompasses many countries]
Yukon River Valley   .Y8    [crosses Canadian-U.S. boundary]
```

Country Cutter used for the country and for everything within it when there is no subarrangement [sec. 3.b]:
```
Italy             .I8
Italy, Southern   .I8
Abruzzi (Italy)   .I8
Naples (Italy)    .I8
```

Country name modified by an adjective, class number represents only that country [sec. 3.c]:
```
Brazil, local, A-Z
  Brazil, North          .N6
  Brazil, Central West   .C4
```

Country name modified by an adjective, class number represents more than one country [sec. 3.c]:
```
South America, by region or country, A-Z
  Brazil, North          .B6
  Brazil, Central West   .B6
```

Latest form of the place name [sec. 4]:
```
.C75 Congo (Democratic Republic)   [not .Z28 Zaire]
.S72 Sri Lanka                     [not .C3 Ceylon]
```

Current political status and name [sec. 5]:
A work on Danzig, Germany, now named Gdańsk, Poland, should be assigned .Pxx for Poland and .Gxx for Gdańsk, even if the work covers only the period when it was called Danzig and located in Germany.

Historical jurisdiction not encompassed by a present-day country [sec. 6]:
```
Islamic Empire   .I742
```

Great Britain with local subarrangement [sec. 7.b]:
A work to which a heading of the type [topic]—England has been assigned should be assigned the Cutter number .Gxx for Great Britain and .Exx for England.
A work to which a heading of the type [topic]—England—London has been assigned should be assigned the Cutter number .Gxx for Great Britain and .Lxx for London.

Island near and treated as part of its parent country [sec. 8.a]:
```
Jutland   .D4   [Denmark]
```

Islands that are countries or isolated from the parent country [sec. 8.b]:
```
Puerto Rico       .P9
Falkland Islands  .F3
```

## Pitfalls
- Do not Cutter for a region or political division inside a country when the caption has no subarrangement; the country Cutter covers it. [sec. 3.b]
- "Brazil, North" gets .N6 only when the class number is for Brazil alone; under a multi-country number it is .B6 (Brazil). [sec. 3.c]
- The former name (Zaire, Ceylon, Danzig) is never the basis for the Cutter, even when the work covers only the earlier period, unless the schedule prints different provisions. [secs. 4-5]
- England, Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland are locals under Great Britain, not countries, unless the schedule provides otherwise. [sec. 7]
- Under an "Under each" pattern, the Local, A-Z line (.x2, .x25) may be used only under individual countries, never under a region; the other lines may be used under regions. [sec. 9]
