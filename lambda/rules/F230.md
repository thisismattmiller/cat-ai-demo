## Applies when

- The work is a yearbook, annual, or similar serial and the schedule shows a parenthesized
  "Yearbooks" number under the topic.
- The work continues a serial already classed in a yearbooks number.

## Rules

1. The LC classification schedules formerly provided separate numbers for yearbooks under many
   major disciplines and topics. In early 1986 the distinction between yearbooks and other
   types of periodicals was largely discontinued (cf. H 2400). With the exception of certain
   areas of class K, the former numbers for yearbooks have been parenthesized and are no longer
   used for new items being cataloged. [Background]
2. General rule. Do not class any newly cataloged titles in parenthesized class numbers for
   yearbooks. Use instead the corresponding number for periodicals. [sec. 1]
3. Serial continuations. When classifying a new serial title that is a continuation of a
   serial already classed in a yearbooks number, assign the same number to the new title that
   was assigned to the earlier title, providing that the numbering of the serial continues.
   [sec. 2]

## Pitfalls

- Class K still has valid yearbook numbers in certain areas; use them there (cf. F 210
  sec. 4.a).
- A continuation only inherits the obsolete yearbooks number when numbering continues;
  otherwise it is a new title and goes in the periodicals number [sec. 2].
