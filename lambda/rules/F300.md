## Applies when

- The schedule for the topic has a class number captioned "Addresses, essays, lectures" (or a
  slight variation).
- The work consists of collected papers, miscellaneous articles, an anthology of readings, or
  similar.

## Rules

1. Where specific numbers exist in the classification schedule with the caption Addresses,
   essays, lectures, use these numbers to class works that consist of collected papers,
   miscellaneous articles, anthologies of readings, etc.
2. In 1986 the subdivision Addresses, essays, lectures was discontinued in subject heading
   practice. That change did not affect classification practice: the class numbers remain in
   use. [Background]

## Pitfalls

- The absence of the subject subdivision "Addresses, essays, lectures" in the 6XX fields does
  not mean the class number is unavailable [Background].
