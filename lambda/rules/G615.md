## Applies when

- The resource is a multipart item (several volumes/parts under one call number) and the call number for each piece needs a volume designation and numbering.
- The resource is a serial whose individual pieces carry volume designations.
- The resource is an analyzed volume of a multipart item or serial (the volume designation comes from the series area, 440/830).

## Rules

1. [sec. 1.a] **New multipart items (not analyzed), in English.** For all new unanalyzed multipart items in English, use the appropriate abbreviation for all designations appearing on the title page and/or the contents note, for example, `vol.` for volume, `pt.` for part, `sect.` for section, `sess.` for session, etc. For a list of approved abbreviations see RDA, Appendix B.
2. [sec. 1.b] **New multipart items (not analyzed), other languages.** For languages other than English, regardless of the vernacular terminology appearing on the title page and/or contents note, use only the following prescribed English pattern: `vol.`, `pt.`, and `no.`
3. [sec. 2] **Established multipart items (not analyzed).** When a pattern for the volume designation/numbering has already been established in the vernacular for a particular call number, continue to use this volume designation/numbering for all added volumes, so that the volume information on each volume is uniformly presented.
4. [sec. 3] **New and established multipart items (analyzed).** For both new and established analyzable multipart items, use the volume designation/numbering provided in the series area of the bibliographic record (440/830 fields).
5. [sec. 4.a] **Serials, not analyzed.** For all serial entries, new and established, that are not analyzed, use the volume designation/numbering determined by the Serial Record Division and indicated in field 362 (Dates of publication and/or volume designation) of the cataloging record.
6. [sec. 4.b] **Serials, analyzed.** For all analyzed serial entries, new or established, use the volume designation/numbering provided in the series area of the bibliographic record (440/830 fields).

## Pitfalls

- A non-English new multipart item never takes a vernacular designation (no `Bd.`, `t.`, `fasc.`): only `vol.`, `pt.`, `no.` [sec. 1.b]. But if a vernacular pattern is already established under the call number, keep it for added volumes [sec. 2].
- For analyzed items (multipart or serial) the designation is taken from the 440/830 series statement, not from the title page [secs. 3, 4.b]; for unanalyzed serials it comes from field 362 [sec. 4.a].
- The volume designation is an integral part of the call number of the item (historically the "small label").
