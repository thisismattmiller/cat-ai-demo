## Applies when
- The first subject heading (600) of the record is the name of a person (the biographee), and the work is a biography, autobiography, diary, collection of letters, collected/selected works, speeches, interviews, or criticism of that person.
- The class number is a biography number (caption "Individual biography, A-Z", "Individual, A-Z" under a Biography caption, or a personal-narratives caption that is part of a Biography caption) and the biographee is on the first Cutter: apply the Biography Table.
- The class number is a biography number but the biographee falls on the second Cutter (first Cutter reserved for a topic, country, etc.): do not apply the Biography Table.
- The class number is a general number (including one whose note says "Including biography" or that lists biographees), or a "Life and reign" or "Personal narratives" number: do not apply the Biography Table.
- The schedule specifies a different table for the biographee (e.g., Table L14, Table E2A): apply that table instead.

## Rules
1. Classification background. Biographical works are classed under the topic with which the biographee is most closely identified; a biography treating one aspect of a many-sided person is classed with that aspect (Jefferson as architect NA737, as musician ML429; several aspects of Jefferson's career in E). A Biography Table may be applied only to an individual biography class number where the name of the biographee is on the first Cutter. For a work to be covered by this chapter, the name of the biographee must be given as the first subject heading. [Background]
2. Kinds of class numbers. [Background]
   - Collective biography: no special rules are required to create Cutter numbers.
   - General class numbers: any class number other than a biography class number. The note "Includes biographies" under a general topic does not make the number a biography number.
   - Biography class numbers: any class number established specifically for biographical works. A collection of an individual's letters or compilation of a person's speeches may be classed in a biography class number. Sometimes separate class numbers are provided for these topics, as well as for autobiography or dictionaries and indexes.
   - Individual biography, A-Z: this caption indicates a biography number, so the Biography Table is used if the biographee is on the first Cutter. Collected works, selected works, autobiographical works, letters, and speeches of the biographee may be classed here.
3. Determining general number vs. biography number. The Biography Table is used only under a biography number in which the biographee is on the first Cutter. Some class numbers look like biography numbers but are not to be treated as such. [sec. 1]
   - a. General numbers are denoted by: [sec. 1.a]
     - The caption indicates a general topic (biographies may be classed there), e.g. D568.4 Arabia [works about T.E. Lawrence as Lawrence of Arabia are classed here].
     - A note indicates that biographies are also classed in that number, but the class number is a general works number, e.g. F53 1771-1865 "Including admission as a state: March 4, 1791. Biography: Jacob Collamer, etc."; HD9011.5 General works. History. "Including biography".
     - Personal narratives numbers, e.g. D811.A3-Z Individual, A-Z under "Personal narratives and other accounts": considered general numbers.
     - "Life and reign" captions in the history schedules: these numbers have special rules: Cutter all works by the 1XX and/or 245 fields, including works by the ruler, e.g. DA539 General works on life and reign of William IV, 1830-1837.
   - b. Biography numbers are denoted by: [sec. 1.b]
     - The caption is Individual biography, A-Z, e.g. Biography: TD139 Collective; TD140 Individual, A-Z.
     - The caption for personal narratives appears as part of a caption for biography, e.g. E444 Biography. Personal narratives of slaves.
4. Shelflisting biographies in biography classes. [sec. 2]
   - a. Individual biographee on the first Cutter: subarrange materials according to the Biography Table unless the schedule provides otherwise. [sec. 2.a]
   - In .xA6-Z, do not Cutter lower than A6. Suggested Cutter numbers for main entries beginning with A: Aa-Af A6-699; Ag-Al A7-799; Am-Ar A8-899; As-Az A9-999. [sec. 2.a]
   - b. Translations: the Translation Table can be applied to the .xA6-Z of the Biography Table. Do not use the Translation Table with the .xA2-.xA5 area of the Biography Table. [sec. 2.b]
   - d. Individual biographee on the second Cutter: when the biography class is established so that the Cutter for the biographee is the second Cutter (the first Cutter is reserved for a topic, country, etc.), do not use the Biography Table. Create a Cutter for the biographee and add a digit or digits to represent the 1XX and/or 245 fields. Arrange autobiographies, etc. and works about the biographee by the 1XX and/or 245 fields. Do not provide any subarrangement such as that provided by the Biography Table. [sec. 2.d]
5. Shelflisting biographies in general classes. [sec. 3]
   - a. General rule: apply the preferred shelflist order (see G 65). File all works about a person after all works by that person. If any works by the biographee have already been shelflisted and a biography is classed in the same class, create a Cutter so that the biography files after the works of the biographee. [sec. 3.a]
   - b. Biography on the first Cutter: establish a first Cutter for the biographee and a second Cutter for the 1XX and/or 245 fields of the biography. [sec. 3.b]
   - c. Biography on the second Cutter: if no works by the biographee exist in that class, establish a Cutter for the biographee and add a digit or digits to represent the 1XX and/or 245 fields. If any works by the biographee have already been shelflisted and a biography is classed in the same class, create a successive Cutter so that the biography files after the works of the biographee and add a digit or digits to represent the 1XX and/or 245 fields. [sec. 3.c]
6. Other tables for biographical works. If an individual class specifies a table other than the Biography Table, apply that table, e.g. LB475.A-Z Individual educators, A-Z, "Subarrange each by Table L14"; E664.A-Z Individual, A-Z, E664.A19 Adams, Charles Francis, 1835-1915 (Table E2A). [sec. 4]

## Tables
BIOGRAPHY TABLE [sec. 2.a] (machine-readable copy: `shelflister/data/biography_table.json`)

| Cutter | Meaning |
|---|---|
| .x | Cutter for the biographee |
| .xA2 | Collected works. By date |
| .xA25 | Selected works. Selections. By date. Including quotations |
| .xA3 | Autobiography, diaries, etc. By date |
| .xA4 | Letters. By date |
| .xA5 | Speeches, essays, and lectures. By date. Including interviews |
| .xA6-Z | Individual biography and criticism. By main entry. Including criticism of selected works, autobiography, quotations, letters, speeches, interviews, etc. |

In .xA6-Z, do not Cutter lower than A6. Suggested Cutter numbers for entries beginning with A:

| Main entry begins | Cutter range |
|---|---|
| Aa-Af | A6-699 |
| Ag-Al | A7-799 |
| Am-Ar | A8-899 |
| As-Az | A9-999 |

                      [first subject heading: King, Martin Luther]

## Pitfalls
- The Biography Table applies only when (1) the number is a biography number and (2) the biographee is on the first Cutter and (3) the biographee is the first subject heading. Fail any one and the table is not used.
- "Includes biographies" / "Including biography" notes and lists of biographees under a period number (F53, HD9011.5) do not make a number a biography number. [sec. 1.a]
- "Personal narratives" numbers (D811.A3-Z) are general numbers; but a personal-narratives caption that is part of a Biography caption (E444) is a biography number. [sec. 1.a, 1.b]
- "Life and reign" numbers: Cutter everything by 1XX and/or 245, including works by the ruler; no Biography Table. [sec. 1.a]
- Translation Table: only in .xA6-Z, never in .xA2-.xA5. [sec. 2.b]
- In .xA6-Z the second Cutter must never fall below A6; main entries beginning Aa-Af are pushed up to A6-699, etc. [sec. 2.a]
- Discontinued practices, not to be used for current cataloging: autobiographies Cuttered .xA3-39 with a title digit [sec. 2.c note]; filing autobiographies before all works about the biographee when the biographee is on the second Cutter [sec. 2.d note].
- When the schedule names a different table (Table L14, Table E2A, etc.), the Biography Table is not used. [sec. 4]
- The schedule overrides the Biography Table whenever it "provides otherwise". [sec. 2.a]
