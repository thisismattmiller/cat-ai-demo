## Applies when
- The class number being applied has a schedule provision for "Documents" (often a reserved Cutter range such as .A1-.A5 or .A1-.A29 Serial / .A3 Nonserial).
- The 1XX is a corporate heading consisting of a jurisdictional name plus agency name as a subheading (e.g., New South Wales. Department of Railways), or a jurisdiction alone; or the work is entered under title with two or more 710 fields containing jurisdictions alone or jurisdictions with an agency-name subheading.
- The work is a serial or a monograph, which determines whether the document number can be used.

## Rules
Government documents are generally not classed as a distinct category of materials; some schedules have provisions for documents, and these rules apply only where such provisions occur. [Background]
1. General rules.
   - a. Treat as documents those works that are entered under a corporate heading consisting of a jurisdictional name plus agency name as a subheading, e.g., New South Wales. Department of Railways, or jurisdiction alone. If the 1XX field is formulated in any other manner, including personal names or headings for government bodies entered under their own names, e.g., Naval Research Laboratory, do not treat the work as a document. Also treat as documents those works that are entered under title, if there are two or more 710 fields containing jurisdictions alone, or jurisdictions with an agency name as a subheading. [sec. 1.a]
   - b. As printed: "Class only serials in document numbers sec. 2, below, applies." (i.e., only serials go in document numbers; sec. 2 governs monographs). [sec. 1.b]
2. Monographs. Do not class monographs in document numbers unless the schedule has explicit provision for monographic documents. [sec. 2]

## Examples
Heading that qualifies as a document [sec. 1.a]:
New South Wales. Department of Railways (jurisdiction plus agency subheading).

Heading that does not qualify as a document [sec. 1.a]:
Naval Research Laboratory (government body entered under its own name).

## Pitfalls
- A government body entered under its own name (e.g., Naval Research Laboratory) is not a document, even though it is governmental. [sec. 1.a]
- A personal-name main entry never makes a document. [sec. 1.a]
- A monograph goes in the Documents range only when the schedule prints a Nonserial line (e.g., .A3); otherwise it goes with General works (e.g., .A6-.Z4). [sec. 2]
