  How to derive the date element of a monograph call number from the 264/260 field (bracketed,
  uncertain, ranged and "between" dates, 1990z decade dates), when to use a different date
  (conferences, multiparts, facsimiles), work letters a/b/c, classes arranged by date, and when
  no date is added (Suppl., Index, date-only classes, updating loose-leafs, date-based legal Cutters).

## Applies when

- Every monograph call number: a date follows the Cutter for the main entry.
- The 264 (or 260) $c is bracketed, questioned, a range, a Roman numeral, "between ... and ...", "not before/after", or absent; the record has a distribution/manufacture/copyright date instead; a note corrects the date.
- The work is entered under a congress/conference heading with a year (111 $d), a corporate body (110), or a preferred title with a date (130); it is a multipart, a facsimile/photocopy, a later edition, or has printing dates in a note.
- The class number is itself arranged by date (schedule says "By date", "By year", "By date of occurrence"), or the item is a Suppl./Index, an updating loose-leaf, or a legal publication whose final Cutter comes from a date span.

## Rules

**Definition.** Publication date refers to the date coded in field 264, second indicator 1, subfield $c. Use the publication date to determine the date in the call number unless instructions to the contrary are provided below. (In 1982 the decision was made to add the publication date to the call number of all monographic works.)

1. **General rule** [sec. 1].
   - a. **Date of publication present** [sec. 1.a]. Add the date of publication to all monographs. Use the four-digit year: the first year of a range; the earliest year of a "between" or "not before" statement; convert Roman numerals; drop brackets and question marks. For "between YYY0 and YYY9" statements (a whole decade, with or without "?"), use the decade with z (1700z, 1990z); if the main entry is a corporate body, use the plain year instead (1700, 1990). See the table under Examples.
   - b. **Date of publication not identified** [sec. 1.b]. If the date of publication is not identified, then use the date of distribution, manufacture, or copyright.
   - c. **Date of publication incorrect** [sec. 1.c]. If the date of publication recorded is incorrect, use the correct date if given.

2. **Special situations** [sec. 2].
   - a. **Congress or conference headings** [sec. 2.a]. If a work is entered under the name of a congress or conference and the year appears in the access point, use the date. If a year is not present, use the publication date (cf. G 230).
   - b. **Multiparts** [sec. 2.b]. Add the date of publication of the first/earliest part (i.e., the earliest date found in either the 264 field or the 362 1# field) to the call number, unless the new multipart item is in a larger multipart item/monographic series classed as a collection. Upon receipt of additional volumes, it may be discovered that the date used in the call number is not the earliest date. Although earlier policy was to reclass to reflect the earlier date, it is no longer necessary to do this.
   - c. **Photocopy or facsimile editions** [sec. 2.c]. If the work is cataloged as a facsimile or photocopy, add the date of the original edition and the work letter a.
   - d. **Other editions** [sec. 2.d]. For later editions of the same work, use the publication date. If another edition is received with the same publication date, use work letters starting with b.
   - e. **Corporate authorship** [sec. 2.e]. For works entered under a corporate body, use the publication date. If another work by the same corporate body is received, use work letters starting with b (cf. G 220).
     - Note: Until 2013 if additional works by the same corporate body were received, they were assigned work letters starting with a.
   - f. **Printing dates in the note area** [sec. 2.f]. Disregard the printing dates in the note area and use the publication date.
   - g. **Date added to preferred title** [sec. 2.g]. When a work is entered under its preferred title and there is a date in the authorized access point, use the publication date in the call number.

3. **Classes arranged by date and subarranged by main entry or other element** [sec. 3]. If a class is subarranged by date and other elements, add the publication date to the number. Follow this instruction even if a date appears as part of the classification number (the schedule date goes in $a after the class number; the Cutter and publication date follow in the usual way).

4. **Exceptions to adding a date** [sec. 4].
   - a. **Supplements and indexes** [sec. 4.a]. Do not add a date to the call number after the designations Suppl. or Index.
   - b. **Classes subarranged by date only** [sec. 4.b]. If a work is classed in an area that is subarranged by date only, do not add another date to the call number. Instead, add a work letter starting with b to the date.
   - c. **Updating loose-leaf materials** [sec. 4.c]. Do not add a date to call numbers for loose-leaf services that are cataloged as such, with "loose-leaf" in subfield $a of the 300 field of the bibliographic record, and continuously kept up to date. (Not all publications with holes punched in pages are "updating loose-leafs." The term "loose-leaf" appearing with an ISBN is not sufficient indication that the publication is an "updating loose-leaf.")
     - Note: Although this rule applies to all classes, the vast majority of such publications are in Class K.
   - d. **Legal publications in which the final Cutter is selected from a span based on dates** [sec. 4.d]. For legal publications in which the final Cutter is selected from a span based on dates, the date is "built in" to the final Cutter and the further addition of a numerical date is superfluous. For example, in Table KFA-KFZ 30.A2-29, the instruction is to subarrange chronologically. The choice of Cutter in the span .A2-29 is based on the date of the publication, and the further addition of a numerical date is unnecessary.
     - Note: In the rare cases where it is necessary to distinguish between different editions of these types of publications, use successive Cutter numbers.

5. **Historical information** [sec. 5]. Until the adoption of RDA in 2013, the publication date was added to the call numbers of monographs by using the principles in the second table under Examples (AACR2 date forms). Use them to interpret dates in older records and existing call numbers.

## Examples

Date of publication not identified; use the date of distribution, manufacture, or copyright (here the 264 #3 manufacture date) [sec. 1.b]:

```
264 #1 $a [Rouiba?] : $b Editions ANEP, $c [date of publication not identified]
264 #3 $a Rouiba : $b ANEP, $c 2010.

use 2010
```

Date of publication incorrect; use the correct date given in a note [sec. 1.c]:

```
264 #1 $a Mars : $b ACME Publishing, $c 3033.
500 ## $a Actually published in 2013.

use 2013
```

Congress or conference heading with a year in the access point; use that year [sec. 2.a]:

```
111 2# $a International Congress of Navigation $n (25th : 1981 : $c Edinburgh, Scotland)

use 1981
```

Later editions with the same publication date; work letters starting with b [sec. 2.d]:

```
.I33 1982
.I33 1982b
.I33 1982c, etc.
```

Another work by the same corporate body with the same publication date; work letters starting with b [sec. 2.e]:

```
.R37 1981
.R37 1981b
.R37 1981c, etc.
```

Printing dates in the note area disregarded; publication date used [sec. 2.f]:

```
260 $c [1982-<1984>
500 $a Vol. 1: 1983 printing; vol. 2: 1984 printing.

use 1982
```

Work entered under preferred title containing a date; use the publication date, not the date in the access point [sec. 2.g]:

```
130 0# $a Census of wholesale trade (1977)
264 1# $a [Washington, D.C.] : $b U.S. Dept. of Commerce, Bureau of the Census $c [1981]

use 1981
```

Class arranged by date (year of the contest) and subarranged by author; the publication date is still added after the Cutter [sec. 3]:

```
In the classification schedule:

     Olympic games. (Modern revivals)
   GV722         Individual contests.  By year
                    Subarrange by author

In the shelflist:

   050 00 $a GV722 1952 $b .W4 1981
```

Class arranged by date and subarranged by place of publication; the publication date is added even though the same date is part of the class number [sec. 3]:

```
In the classification schedule:

     King James, Authorized version (1611)
   BS185     Texts.  By date
                Subarrange by place of publication

In the shelflist:

   050 00 $a BS185 1984 $b .N37 1984
```

Class arranged by date of occurrence, subarranged by place, A-Z, and by author, A-Z; publication date added after the author Cutter [sec. 3]:

```
In the classification schedule:

     Earthquakes
   HV600         By date of occurrence
                    Subarrange by place, A-Z, and by author, A-Z

In the shelflist:

   050 00 $a HV600 2001 .E47 $b U55 2001
```

Supplements and indexes: no date after Suppl. or Index; numbered supplements/indexes take the number [sec. 4.a]:

```
.C64 1977
.C64 1977 Suppl.
.C64 1977 Suppl. 2
```

```
.C64 1977
.C64 1977 Index
.C64 1977 Index 2
```

Class subarranged by date only: no second date; a second item with the same date takes work letter b [sec. 4.b]:

```
In the classification schedule:

   Tennyson, Alfred Lord
   PR5551     Selected works.  By date

In the shelflist:

   050 00 $a PR5551 $b 1968
   050 00 $a PR5551 $b 1968b
```

Legal publication whose final Cutter is chosen from a date-based span (Table KFA-KFZ 30.A2-29, subarranged chronologically); no numerical date is added after the Cutter [sec. 4.d]:

```
050 00 $a KFV2430 1950 $b .A243
```

## Pitfalls

- The date is taken from 264 _1 $c (publication), not from copyright, distribution or manufacture unless the publication date is not identified [sec. 1.b]; never from printing dates in notes [sec. 2.f].
- Ranges and "between/not before/not after" statements always yield the earliest year, except whole-decade "between YYY0 and YYY9" statements, which yield a decade date with z (1990z), and even those become a plain year (1990) when the main entry is a corporate body [sec. 1.a; sec. 5 for 197-, 19--].
- A conference heading's year (111 $d) overrides the publication date; a preferred title's year (130) does not [secs. 2.a, 2.g].
- Facsimiles/photocopies use the date of the original plus work letter a; other same-date editions and same-date corporate works use work letters starting with b (before 2013, corporate works started with a) [secs. 2.c, 2.d, 2.e].
- Multiparts: earliest date in 264 or 362 1#; do not reclass if an earlier volume later arrives; and do not add a date when the multipart is inside a larger set classed as a collection [sec. 2.b].
- When the class number itself contains a date (GV722 1952, BS185 1984, HV600 2001 .E47), the publication date is still added after the Cutter; when the class is subarranged by date *only* (PR5551), the date is the whole item number and duplicates get b, c ... [secs. 3, 4.b].
- No date after Suppl. or Index; no date for updating loose-leafs (300 $a "loose-leaf", continuously updated); no date when a legal Cutter span already encodes the date, where different editions are distinguished by successive Cutters instead [sec. 4].
