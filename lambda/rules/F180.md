## Applies when

- The candidate class number is printed in the schedule in angle brackets, e.g. `<BM450>`,
  `<PS8001-8599>`.

## Rules

1. Occasionally, "alternative" or "reserved" class numbers are included in the schedules. They
   are not assigned to works in the Library of Congress' collections, but are provided for the
   convenience of other libraries, which may use them to serve the needs of their users. The
   numbers are shown in angle brackets. [Background]

## Examples

Reserved number for a complete text:
`<BM450>  Complete text of the Tanakh`

Reserved range for a national literature:
`<PS8001-8599>  Canadian literature`

## Pitfalls

- Angle-bracketed numbers are not obsolete numbers (those are parenthesized; see F 185); they
  are optional numbers for other libraries and are never used in LC call numbers.
