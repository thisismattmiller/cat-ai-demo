## Applies when
- The class number is in the N schedule and the schedule directs use of Table N6 (form caption "Special artists, A-Z", artist on the first Cutter) or Table N7 (first Cutter is a region or country, artist on the second Cutter).
- The work is by or about an individual artist (or a family or firm treated as a special artist): autobiography, letters, speeches/essays/interviews, reproductions or exhibition catalogs of the artist's work, individual works of art, biography, criticism.
- Do NOT apply this card to art critics, art historians, art forgers, art thieves, art restorers, art patrons, or art dealers; they are not artists. Critical and biographical works about them follow the general biography rules and the Biography Table in G 320. [Background]

## Rules
1. Works on individual artists are classed according to the medium described in the work being cataloged; a single artist may therefore be classed in several different numbers in the N schedule. General biographies and criticism of artists who work in several media are classed in N. Special individuals, families, or firms may be considered as special artists. [Background]
2. Table N6 is completely different from the Biography Table in G 320. Even though the notation bears a superficial resemblance, none of the Cutters has the same meaning (e.g., .xA2 is Autobiography in N6 but Collected works in the Biography Table). [Background]
3. Identification of biographical works: follow the procedures given in G 320. [sec. 1]
4. Biography tables. Use the tables below to subarrange works by and about individual artists throughout the N schedule. [sec. 2]
   - a. Table N6: use where indicated in the N schedule, for classes with the form caption Special artists, A-Z. The first Cutter number designates the artist (e.g., ND623.L5, Leonardo da Vinci). The second Cutter number is selected from the subarrangement in the table. [sec. 2.a]
   - b. Table N7: use where indicated in the N schedule, when the first Cutter number represents a region or country, and the artist is represented by a second Cutter number; the table's x, x2, x3-39 are digits appended to the artist's Cutter. [sec. 2.b]
5. Translations. Apply the Translation Table to only two categories in Table N6 and one category in Table N7: in Table N6 apply it to .xA6-79 and to .xA8-Z; in Table N7 to .x3-39. Do not use the Translation Table with the captions in the artist tables that are arranged by date, i.e., .xA2 through .xA4 in Table N6, and x and x2 in Table N7. [sec. 3]
6. Obsolete table. The individual artist table used prior to November 22, 1977 (below) is obsolete and is included only as a point of reference for interpreting older shelflist entries. [sec. 4]

## Tables
Table N6 (First Cutter number for artist) [sec. 2.a] (machine-readable copy: `shelflister/data/artists_table.json`, key "N6")

| Cutter | Meaning |
|---|---|
| .xA2 | Autobiography. By date |
| .xA3 | Letters. By date |
| .xA35 | Speeches, essays, interviews, etc. of the artist. By date |
| .xA4 | Reproductions (Collections). By date. Including exhibition catalogs [even if the main entry is not the artist] |
| .xA6-79 | Individual works of art. Alphabetically by title of work of art and date |
| .xA8-Z | Biography and criticism |

Table N7 (Second Cutter number for artist) [sec. 2.b] (key "N7")

| Cutter | Meaning |
|---|---|
| x | Autobiography, letters, speeches, essays, interviews, etc. By date |
| x2 | Reproductions. By date. Including collections, individual works of art, exhibition catalogs [even if the main entry is not the artist] |
| x3-39 | Biography and criticism. Alphabetically by main entry |

Obsolete ARTISTS (CLASS N) table, used prior to November 22, 1977; reference only [sec. 4] (key "obsolete_artists_table_pre_1977")

| Cutter | Meaning |
|---|---|
| .xA2 | Autobiography. By title |
| .xA3 | Letters. By title |
| .xA35 | Miscellaneous. Speeches, essays, etc. By title |
| .xA4-59 | Reproductions cataloged under artist's name when there is no editor. By title |
| .xA6-Z | Reproductions (by editor); Catalogs (by editor or corporate body responsible for preparation); Biography and criticism (by main entry); Title entries (by title); etc. |

## Examples
Artist on the first Cutter under a "Special artists, A-Z" caption; second Cutter from Table N6 [sec. 2.a]:
```
ND623.L5    Leonardo da Vinci
```

## Pitfalls
- Never carry Biography Table meanings into Table N6: .xA2 = Autobiography (not collected works), .xA3 = Letters (not autobiography), .xA4 = Reproductions (not letters). [Background]
- Reproductions and exhibition catalogs go in .xA4 (N6) / x2 (N7) even if the main entry is not the artist. [sec. 2]
- In Table N6 individual works of art occupy .xA6-79 and biography/criticism begins at .xA8, not .xA6 as in the Biography Table. [sec. 2.a]
- Translation Table only in .xA6-79 and .xA8-Z (N6) and x3-39 (N7); never in the by-date categories .xA2-.xA4 (N6) or x, x2 (N7). [sec. 3]
- Critics, historians, forgers, thieves, restorers, patrons and dealers are not artists: use G 320 and the Biography Table. [Background]
- The pre-1977 table arranged by title and used .xA4-59 for reproductions; do not apply it to current cataloging. [sec. 4]
