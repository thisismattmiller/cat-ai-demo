## Applies when
- The resource is a collection of abstracts (summaries that present the essential points of the original publications, usually including the author's conclusion; give enough detail to decide whether to consult the original; or evaluate or criticize the publication).
- Distinguish from an annotated bibliography, whose annotations usually give only a general indication of the subject matter of each publication and are seldom critical; reference to the original is normally required to determine usefulness. Annotated bibliographies are classed in Z.
- The author's or publisher's intention, as expressed in the title, preface, etc., may serve as a secondary factor in deciding which of the two an item is.

## Rules
For classification purposes, a collection of abstracts may be regarded as a collection of miscellaneous works and is normally classed with the Collected works number under the topic in question. [Background]
1. Schedules with provisions for abstracts. If the schedule provides specifically for abstracts, class with the appropriate number. Such numbers are extremely rare in the LC classification system. [sec. 1]
2. Schedules without provisions for abstracts. If the schedule does not provide specifically for abstracts, class with the Collected works (nonserial) number if such a number or its equivalent exists. [sec. 2]
3. Using the General works number. If the schedule does not provide special form captions, and there is insufficient material on the topic to justify a new caption, class with the General works number. [sec. 3]
4. Abstracts in serial form. Class abstracts published in serial form with the number for periodicals. [sec. 4]

## Pitfalls
- Annotated bibliographies are classed in Z, not with the topic's Collected works number; decide primarily on the nature of the summaries or annotations. [Background]
- Numbers specifically for abstracts are extremely rare; expect to use Collected works (nonserial) or General works. [sec. 1]
- Serial abstracts take the periodicals number, not the Collected works number. [sec. 4]
