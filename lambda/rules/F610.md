## Applies when

- The resource is an index (245/5XX describe it as an index, concordance, or key) to one publication, to a serial, to the works of one author, to the publications of one organization, or to a set of two or more books or serials on a topic.

## Rules

1. Indexes to individual publications (including serials). Class with the work being indexed. [sec. 1]
2. Indexes to the publications of an individual author. Class in the biography and criticism number for that author (or in a more specific number under the author, if such a number is provided). [sec. 2]
3. Indexes to publications of an individual society, organization, etc. Class in Z1201-7999 with bibliographies on the topic in which the organization specializes. [sec. 3]
4. Comprehensive indexes to lists of two or more books or serials on a single topic. Class as bibliography on the topic in Z1201-7999, K, or M. [sec. 4]

## Pitfalls

- An index to a single work or serial never gets its own topical number; it takes the number of the thing indexed [sec. 1]. F 720 sec. 3 governs whether `Suppl.` is added to that call number.
- An author index goes to the author's biography and criticism number, not to a bibliography number in Z [sec. 2].
