## Applies when
- The resource is a map, an atlas, or other cartographic material.
- The resource combines text and cartographic content, and it must be decided which is primary.
- The resource is a separate text supplementary to specific maps.
- The resource is about maps, atlases, map collections, map making, map reading, geography, travel, voyages or exploration.

## Rules
1. Cartographic materials. The following are classified in G1000-G9980 [sec. 1]:
   - Any work that is clearly a map or atlas.
   - Works with both textual and cartographic content, in which the primary emphasis is on the cartographic content and the textual content is secondary or complementary, serving mainly to support and explain the cartographic content.
   - Separate texts that are clearly identified as supplementary to specific maps, including explanatory texts intended for use with the sheets of collected, quadrangle-format map series.
2. Books and serials about geography/cartography or with secondary cartographic content. The following are classified in G1-G922, GA, or other appropriate classes, according to their subject content [sec. 2]:
   - Works about maps and atlases, including works about map collections, map libraries, map cataloging, map making, map reading, bibliographies of maps, etc.
   - Works with both textual and cartographic content, in which the primary emphasis is on the textual content and the cartographic content is secondary, serving mainly to support, illustrate, or explain the textual content.
   - Works about geography and related subjects, such as travel, voyages, exploration, etc.

## Pitfalls
- Works with cartographic content are classed as books or serials only if the cartographic content is secondary to the textual content. [Background]
- A separate explanatory text for a map series is classed with the maps (G1000-G9980), not as a book, even though it is text. [sec. 1]
- Works about maps (map bibliographies, map libraries, map making) are books in G1-G922 or GA, not maps. [sec. 2]
