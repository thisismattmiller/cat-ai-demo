## Applies when

- The subject headings are of the type Library resources, Archival resources, or use the subdivisions --Library resources / --Archival resources: works that describe the resources of a single library or archival collection, or of various collections as a group, with or without topical orientation.
- Not for: bibliographies, library catalogs, union lists (they list the works); archival inventories (F 495); works on special collections (Libraries--Special collections--[topic or form], which are about setting up and maintaining a collection); directories of special libraries or archives (general institutional information rather than research opportunities).

## Rules

1. Resources of one library or archive. [sec. 1]
   a. Without topical orientation. Class with the particular library in Z733+ or with the particular archive in CD. [sec. 1.a]
   b. With topical orientation. Class with the topic in national or subject bibliography in Z1201+. [sec. 1.b]

2. Resources of more than one library or archive. [sec. 2]
   a. Without topical orientation. Class with the region in which the group of libraries or archives discussed is located in Z729+ or in CD. [sec. 2.a]
   b. With topical orientation. Class with the topic in national or subject bibliography in Z1201+. [sec. 2.b]

3. Works on the organization and resources of one library or archive. Class works that discuss both the organization and particular resources of a single library collection with the topic in Z688. [sec. 3]

4. Directories of multiple libraries or archives, with information on their resources. Class directories that list both the libraries or archives devoted to a special topic and provide significant information on collection resources with the particular category of library or archive in Z675 or in CD. [sec. 4]

## Pitfalls

- Topical orientation sends the work to Z1201+ (subject bibliography) even though it is not itself a bibliography [sec. 1.b, 2.b].
- A work that also covers the organization of the library goes to Z688, not Z733+ [sec. 3].
- A directory with significant resource information goes to Z675 (or CD), not Z729+ [sec. 4].
