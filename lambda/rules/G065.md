## Applies when

- Several works by (or about) the same individual author fall in a single class number and the schedule gives no contrary subarrangement.
- The item is a collected/selected works, a translation, a selection or abridgment, an edition or reprint, a facsimile or photocopy, criticism of a specific work, or biography/criticism of the author, and its Cutter must be placed relative to the author's other works.

## Rules

1. After consulting the classification schedule for special instructions, examine the shelflist to determine the filing position of the work in hand according to the LC filing rules (see G 100) and the table below.
2. Unless there are provisions to the contrary in the particular schedule being applied, the works of an individual author in a single class number are arranged by category of publication using a series of successive Cutter numbers.
3. Apply the table: the author's base Cutter (e.g. .L54) is used for collected works; selected works take the next successive Cutter (.L542); each separate work takes its own successive Cutter by title (.L55, .L56 ...); and within a separate work the Cutter is expanded (12-19 translation, 2 selection, 212-219 translation of selection, 3 or 3-39 criticism) and combined with the date as shown.
4. The double-Cuttered examples (marked with *) show arrangement on the second Cutter: when the author is already the second Cutter (e.g. .S6L552), the same expansions are applied to that second Cutter.

## Tables

Preferred Shelflist Order - Individual Authors. When the works of an individual author are filed in a single class number, they are arranged in the following order:

| Category | Arrangement | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Collected works | By date | .L54 1966 |
| — Translations | By date | .L5412-5419 1986 |
| Selected works | By date | .L542 1986 |
| — Translations | By date | .L54212-54219 1986 |
| Separate works | By title | |
| — Original work | Cutter and date | .L55 1952 |
| — Facsimile or photocopy of original work | Cutter and date with a | .L55 1952a (.L55 1952aa, ab, etc.) |
| — Edition or reprint | Cutter and date | .L55 1967 |
| — Facsimile or photocopy of edition | Cutter and date of edition with a | .L55 1967a (.L55 1967aa, ab, etc.) |
| — Translation | Cutter expanded by 12-19 and date | .L5513 1963 [English translation] |
| — Selection, abridgement, or condensed version | Cutter expanded by 2 and date | .L552 1981; *.S6L552 1981 |
| — Translation of selection, abridgement, or condensed version | Cutter expanded by 212-219 and date | .L55213 1982 [English translation] |
| — Adaptations | By adapter, A-Z [used only for works cataloged prior to AACR 2. Adaptations are now classed with works of adapter] | |
| — Criticism | Cutter expanded by 3 or 3-39 and date | .L553T5 1976; *.S6L5537 1976 (see G 340, #3) |
| Biography and criticism | By author, A-Z | .L56B78 1986 |

*The double Cuttered examples show arrangement on the second Cutter.

## Examples

The examples are the right-hand column of the table above; the sequence they produce in the shelflist is:

```
.L54 1966              collected works
.L5412-5419 1986       translations of collected works
.L542 1986             selected works
.L54212-54219 1986     translations of selected works
.L55 1952              separate work, original
.L55 1952a             facsimile or photocopy of the original (.L55 1952aa, ab, etc.)
.L55 1967              edition or reprint
.L55 1967a             facsimile or photocopy of that edition (.L55 1967aa, ab, etc.)
.L5513 1963            English translation (translations 12-19)
.L552 1981             selection, abridgement, or condensed version
.L55213 1982           English translation of the selection (212-219)
.L553T5 1976           criticism of the separate work (3 or 3-39), second Cutter for critic
.L56B78 1986           biography and criticism of the author, second Cutter for author of the biography
```

Double-Cuttered form, arrangement on the second Cutter (the author is L55 after a first Cutter .S6):

```
.S6L552 1981           selection, abridgement, or condensed version
.S6L5537 1976          criticism (see G 340, #3)
```

## Pitfalls

- A schedule provision to the contrary overrides this table [Background].
- Facsimiles and photocopies keep the date of the original (or of the edition reproduced) and add work letter a; further facsimiles of the same take aa, ab, etc.
- Translations expand the Cutter by 12-19 (not 1x with a single digit), leaving 12-19 for languages; translations of selections use 212-219.
- Criticism of a separate work expands by 3 (or a 3-39 range) and then takes a second Cutter for the critic (.L553T5); when the author is already the second Cutter, the digit is appended to that second Cutter (.S6L5537).
- "Adaptations, by adapter A-Z" applies only to works cataloged prior to AACR 2; adaptations are now classed with the works of the adapter.
- Biography and criticism of the author as a whole takes the next successive Cutter after the separate works (.L56) with a second Cutter for the biographer.
