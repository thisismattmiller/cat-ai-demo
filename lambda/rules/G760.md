## Applies when

- A legal material (class K) is kept up to date by revised, recompiled, or replacement volumes, identified by a note in the bibliographic record, e.g., "Kept up to date by replacement volumes."
- A new edition of a legal material supersedes an earlier volume or edition.

## Rules

1. Superseded materials are volumes or editions that have been revised, recompiled, or replaced by later dated volumes or editions.
2. New editions are cataloged (and shelflisted) as usual; the replaced volumes themselves receive no new call number treatment.
3. Materials that are kept up to date by revised, recompiled, or replacement volumes are identified by a note in the bibliographic record, e.g., "Kept up to date by replacement volumes."

## Pitfalls

- The chapter distinguishes superseded volumes (revised, recompiled, or replacement volumes under an existing record, flagged by a "Kept up to date by ..." note) from new editions, which are cataloged as usual; only the latter get a new call number (for the dating of editions see G 145).
