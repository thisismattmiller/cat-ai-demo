  How to Cutter collections and selections of an author's nonfiction works entered under an
  RDA conventional collective title (240 Works / Essays / Works. Selections): one Cutter for
  all collections, the next successive Cutter for all selections, each subarranged by date.

## Applies when

- The resource is a collection or selection of nonfiction (non-literary) works of one author, entered under a personal author with an RDA conventional collective title in the 240 (e.g., `240 10 $a Essays`, `240 10 $a Works. $k Selections`, `240 10 $a Essays. $k Selections`).
- Not for compilations by literary authors (see F 633).

## Rules

1. [sec. 1] **General rule.** Unless the schedule has specific instructions to the contrary, follow the preferred shelflist order (cf. G 65).
2. [sec. 2] **Subarrangement of collections and selections.** Assign a single Cutter number to all collections. Also assign a single successive Cutter number to all selections. Subarrange collections and selections by the date of publication, and a work letter if necessary (cf. G 140).
   - Collections (240 `Works`, `Essays`, etc., without `$k Selections`) share one Cutter (e.g., .W57).
   - Selections (240 with `$k Selections`) share the next successive Cutter (e.g., .W572), regardless of which collective title the selections carry.
   - An individual work by the same author receives its own Cutter, filed after the selections Cutter (e.g., .W58).

## Examples

Definition example, an author's non-literary compilation entered under a conventional collective title:

```
100 1# $a Prontera, Francesco.
240 10 $a Essays. $k Selections
245 10 $a Geografia e storia nella Grecia antica / $c Francesco Prontera.
```

Hypothetical example [sec. 3]. Collection (240 Essays): single Cutter for collections:

```
050 00 $a D1051 $b .W57 2013
100 1# $a Wilder, Julie, $d 1940-2010.
240 10 $a Essays
245 10 $a Postwar Europe : $b the essays of Julie Wilder.
264 1# $a 2013.
```

Selection (240 Works. Selections): successive Cutter shared by all selections, subarranged by date:

```
050 00 $a D1051 $b .W572 2009
100 1# $a Wilder, Julie, $d 1940-2010.
240 10 $a Works. $k Selections
245 10 $a Thoughts on postwar Europe / $c Julie Wilder.
264 1# $a 2009.
```

Selection (240 Essays. Selections): same selections Cutter, later date:

```
050 00 $a D1051 $b .W572 2012
100 1# $a Wilder, Julie, $d 1940-2010.
240 10 $a Essays. $k Selections
245 12 $a Europe since 1945 : $b twelve essays  / $c Julie Wilder.
264 1# $a 2012.
```

Individual work by the same author, no collective title: own Cutter after the compilations:

```
050 00 $a D1051 $b .W58 2010
100 1# $a Wilder, Julie, $d 1940-2010.
245 10 $a Western Europe since 1945 / $c Julie Wilder.
264 1# $a 2010.
```

## Pitfalls

- Schedule-specific instructions override the preferred shelflist order [sec. 1].
- All selections share one Cutter even when their collective titles differ (`Works. Selections` and `Essays. Selections` both at .W572 in the example); do not give each collective title its own Cutter.
- Same-year collections or selections are distinguished by a work letter on the date (G 140).
- Literary authors' compilations are subarranged under F 633, not this chapter.
