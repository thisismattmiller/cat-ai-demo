  How to build the call number for works entered under a conference heading (Cutter for the
  conference name or for the higher body, date of the conference from the heading, work
  letters b, c, ..., ba, bb for same-date publications), for conferences not treated as
  creator, multi-year conferences, headings without number/date/place, conference serials,
  and commentaries on conferences (z work letters or double Cutter).

## Applies when

- The 1XX is a conference heading (111), either established directly under its name (e.g., `International Congress of Comparative Law`) or subordinately to a higher body (e.g., `American Academy of Forensic Sciences. Annual Meeting`).
- The resource is the proceedings/outcome of a conference but the conference is not considered the creator (entered under title) [sec. 3].
- The resource is a commentary on, or a history of, a specific conference or a named series of conferences [sec. 7].
- The resource is a supplement or index to a work entered under a conference heading [sec. 8].

## Rules

1. [sec. 1.a] **Conferences established directly under their names.** Establish a Cutter number for the name of the conference. Complete the call number by adding the date of the conference that appears in the heading (not the publication date).
2. [sec. 1.b] **Conferences established subordinately to a higher body.** Establish a Cutter number for the name of the higher body. Ignore the name of the subordinately-entered conference, following the general principle about subheadings in non-jurisdictional name headings (cf. G 220 sec. 3). Complete the call number by adding the date of the conference that appears in the heading.
3. [sec. 1.c] **Headings without dates.** If the heading does not include the date of the conference, use the publication date to complete the call number.
4. [sec. 1.d] **Works with the same heading.** Assign the same Cutter number to works with the same conference heading, including translations, selections, editions, etc. (No Translation Table.)
5. [sec. 2.a] **More than one publication in a given year, general rule.** Formulate the call number for individual works with the same conference date by adding work letters `b`, `c`, etc., to the date in the call number. Do not use the single letter `z` for this purpose (for use of `z` see sec. 7.a).
6. [sec. 2.b] **Single work letters.** When assigning a work letter to the date, assign the next unused single letter.
7. [sec. 2.c] **Double work letters.** If all single work letters have been used, add double letters to the date in the call number (`1975ba`, `1975bb`, ...).
8. [sec. 3] **Conference not considered to be creator.** If the conference is not considered to be the creator of the resource, Cutter for the title and not the conference. Use the publication date to complete the call number even if the place and date appear in the title.
9. [sec. 4] **Multi-year conferences.** If a conference occurs over a period of years, use the earliest date in the call number. (Until 2013, the latest date was used.)
10. [sec. 5.a] **Conference headings without numbering, date, and place, general rule.** When a conference heading lacks numbering, date, and place, follow the principles for corporate bodies (cf. G 220). Use the same Cutter, add the publication date, and a work letter if appropriate.
11. [sec. 5.b] **Identical headings, some with fuller information.** When a heading for a series of conferences is the same, except that the structure of the heading in some entries contains the number, the year, and the place, use successive Cutter numbers (one Cutter for the bare heading, the next successive Cutter for the heading with number/year/place).
12. [sec. 6] **Comparisons between monographs and serials.** When a congress publishes both monographic and serial works, use successive Cutter numbers for serials and monographs. For serials, add a work letter to the Cutter starting with the first serial entry (`a`, `b`, ...), following the principles for corporate bodies (cf. G 220). For monographs, add a date to the Cutter for the monograph, and then follow the principles of sec. 2.a.
13. [sec. 7.a] **Commentaries on specific conferences.** If the work is a commentary on a specific conference, use the Cutter number of the conference, the date of the conference and a work letter or combination of work letters starting with `z`, `za`, `zb`, etc. These works usually have a conference as the first subject.
14. [sec. 7.b] **Commentaries on conferences without specific years.** If the work is about a named conference in general (no specific year given), use the Cutter number of the conference, then:
    - [sec. 7.b(1)] **Conference on the first Cutter.** If the first Cutter represents the conference, assign a second Cutter to represent the 1XX field and/or 240 or 245 fields. Use the publication date to complete the call number.
    - [sec. 7.b(2)] **Conference on the second Cutter.** If the second Cutter represents the conference, expand the second Cutter by adding a digit or digits to represent the 1XX and/or 245 fields. Use the publication date to complete the call number.
15. [sec. 7.c] **Conferences entered under title.** Cutter works about a conference that was originally entered under a title by using the Cutter of the original title and then follow the provisions in G 340 sec. 1-2. Use the publication date to complete the call number.
16. [sec. 7.d] **Commentary on a conference not in LC collections.** Determine where the conference would class by following the instructions in F 240, and then apply the provisions of sec. 7.a-d.
17. [sec. 8.a] **Supplements.** Use the call number of the original work plus `Suppl.` for conference entries designated by the cataloger as supplements (cf. F 720).
18. [sec. 8.b] **Indexes.** For indexes to serials and all works entered under a conference heading, use the call number of the original work and add `Index` (cf. F 720).

## Examples

Conference established directly; Cutter for the conference, date of the conference from the heading (not the publication date) [sec. 1.a]:

```
Main entry:  Western Hemisphere Energy Symposium (1st : 1979 : McLean, Va.)
Title/date:  Summary of the Western Hemisphere ... [1980]

050 00 $a TJ163.25.A45 $b W47 1979

Main entry:  Western Hemisphere Energy Symposium (2nd : 1980 : Rio de
Janeiro, Brazil)
Title/date:  Summary of the Second Western ... [1981]

050 00 $a TJ163.25.A45 $b W47 1980
```

Conference subordinate to a higher body; Cutter for the higher body, conference date from the heading [sec. 1.b]:

```
Main entry:  American Academy of Physical Education. Annual Meeting (61st :
1989 : Boston, Mass.)
Title/date:  The evolving undergraduate major ... [1990]

050 00 $a GV365 $b .A47 1989

Main entry:  American Academy of Physical Education. Annual Meeting (62nd :
1990 : New Orleans, La.)
Title/date:  New possibilities, new paradigms? ... [1991]

050 00 $a GV365 $b .A47 1990
```

Same conference heading, including a Russian translation; same Cutter [sec. 1.d]:

```
Main entry:  International Conference on Vacuum Metallurgy (4th : 1973 :
Tokyo, Japan)
Title/date:  Proceedings of the fourth International ... 1974.

050 00 $a TN686.5.V3 $b I49 1973

Main entry:  International Conference on Vacuum Metallurgy (7th : 1982 :
Tokyo, Japan)
Uniform title:  Proceedings.... Russian
Title:  Materialy VII Mezhdunarodnoi ... 1984.

050 00 $a TN686.5.V3 $b I49 1982
```

```

Subject: Encuentro Nacional de Mujeres (Argentina)—History.

050 00 $a HQ1532.E54 $b A46 2009
```

Commentary on a named conference in general, conference on the second Cutter; second Cutter expanded with a digit for the main entry, publication date [sec. 7.b(2)]:

```

Title/date:  40 years of the RAC … 1992.
Subject: Retail Advertising Conference—History.

050 00 $a HF5813.U5 $b R483 1992
```

Commentary on a conference originally entered under title; Cutter of the original title, then G 340 [sec. 7.c]:

```

Title/date:  What is the effect of ... 1984.

050 00 $a TA1673.E93 $b B34 1984
```

## Pitfalls

- The date in the call number for a conference heading is the *conference* date from the heading, not the imprint date [sec. 1.a–b]; use the publication date only when the heading has no date [sec. 1.c], when the conference is not the creator [sec. 3], for headings lacking number/date/place [sec. 5.a], and for commentaries on unnumbered/undated conferences [sec. 7.b–c].
- For subordinately entered conferences, Cutter for the higher body and ignore the conference subheading [sec. 1.b].
- Different numbered meetings held in the same year collide on the date: distinguish with work letters `b`, `c`, ... then `ba`, `bb`, ... Never use single `z` as an ordinary work letter; `z`, `za`, `zb` are reserved for commentaries [secs. 2.a, 7.a]. (For legal materials the double-letter sequence starts at `za`; see G 750 sec. 4.)
- Multi-year conferences: earliest year since 2013 (older entries may show the latest year) [sec. 4].
- A bare heading and the same heading with number/year/place are Cuttered successively, not identically [sec. 5.b]; serials and monographs of the same congress are also on successive Cutters (serials with work letters, monographs with dates) [sec. 6] — unlike corporate bodies in G 220 sec. 11, which share one Cutter.
- Translations, selections, editions under the same conference heading share the Cutter; the Translation Table is not applied [sec. 1.d].
- The sec. 2.b example is printed in the manual as `050 00 $a $a RD78.4 $b .W67 1980d` (doubled `$a`); the intended call number is `RD78.4 .W67 1980d`.
