## Applies when

- The topic's schedule has a "Collected works (nonserial)" number at its head, and the work is
  a nonserial collection: a multipart item of monographic works by several authors, one
  author's collected works on the topic, or a collection of abstracts.
- The work is the selected works of one author on the topic.

## Rules

1. General rule. Class the following types of publications in the Collected works (nonserial)
   number found at the head of major topics: [sec. 1]
   - Multipart items consisting of monographic works by more than one author.
   - Collected works of one author, e.g., the complete works of one author on the topic,
     including monographs, essays, etc.
   - Collections of abstracts (cf. F 480).
2. Selected works of one author. Class the selected works of one author on a topic in the
   General works number. [sec. 2]
3. Using the General works number. If there is no provision for nonserial collected works
   under a topic, do not establish a new number. Use the General works number instead.
   [sec. 3]
4. Serial publications. Class serial publications in the periodicals number. [sec. 4]

## Pitfalls

- "Collected works" (complete) and "selected works" of one author go to different numbers:
  Collected works (nonserial) vs. General works [sec. 1-2].
- Never propose a new Collected works (nonserial) number; fall back to General works [sec. 3].
