## Applies when

- The work is published by the Library of Congress or one of its departments or offices (110/260/264 name the Library of Congress or one of its divisions as publisher).
- Not applicable to works merely sponsored or compiled by the Library of Congress but published elsewhere; those are not official publications of the Library.

## Rules

1. Class number for the subject of the work. Assign the appropriate class number for the work being cataloged. [sec. 1]
   - If the work classes in the Z schedule, assign also an alternate number, if appropriate, following the instructions in F 150 for providing these numbers.

2. Class number for Library of Congress division or office. Assign an additional number for the division or office responsible for the publication from the special section for Library of Congress publications located at Z663-Z663.99. [sec. 2]
   - This is not an alternate number. It is a number that appears in a separate 051 field and that is actually assigned to a copy of the work.
   - If a new number is needed in this area, submit a proposal to establish it.

## Examples

(The chapter contains no call-number examples.)

## Pitfalls

- The Z663-Z663.99 number goes in 051, not in a second $a of 050; it is not an alternate number [sec. 2].
- Formerly, copies assigned to Z633-Z633.9 were housed in the Rare Book Division; that practice was discontinued in 2015 and the copies are now in the General Collections [sec. 2, note].
- A work sponsored or compiled by LC but published elsewhere does not get the Z663 number (BACKGROUND).
