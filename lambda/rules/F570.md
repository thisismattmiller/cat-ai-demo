## Applies when
- The resource is a commentary on an individual work (not a commentary on an author's output as a whole).
- The original work is a classical text, sacred work, or a work by an individual literary author, in which case schedule-specific practices apply.

## Rules
1. General rule. Classify commentaries with the original text in the original language. If the full call number for the original work is not available, create such a number so that it can be expanded according to G 340. [sec. 1]
2. Special provisions in the schedules. Many varying practices for the treatment of commentaries are provided in the individual schedules, especially for classical authors, sacred works, etc. Such specific provisions in the schedules, as well as any practices that have been developed in specific areas, take precedence over the general rule and should continue to be followed. [sec. 2]
3. Commentaries on the works of individual literary authors: see F 632. [sec. 3]
4. Shelflisting. For shelflisting of commentaries, see G 340. [sec. 4]

## Pitfalls
- The commentary goes with the original text in its original language, even if the commentary is in another language. [sec. 1]
- Schedule provisions and established practices for classical authors, sacred works, etc. override the general rule. [sec. 2]
